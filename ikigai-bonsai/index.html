Privacy Policy — Ikigai Bonsai

Last updated: 10/12/2026

This policy applies to the Android version of Ikigai Bonsai, distributed through Google Play.

Summary

We don't collect any data from you. Ikigai Bonsai has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your trees, care history, streaks, coins, achievements, settings and decoration choices are saved only on your device. They are never sent to BomCo, to any server we operate, or to any third party. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Network use

The app works entirely offline. Its fonts are built into the app, and it makes no network requests of its own, so no information is sent from your device to us or to anyone else while you use it.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Advertising, analytics and sharing

The app contains no advertising or analytics code. We do not sell, rent or share your data.

Children

Ikigai Bonsai is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
